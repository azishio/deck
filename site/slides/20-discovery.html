The file system is the outline slides/ ├── 00-title.html ├── 10-background.html ├── 20-architecture/ │ ├── 00-overview.html │ └── 10-ingestion.html └── 90-summary.html

Order comes from the path, sorted lexicographically — no natural sort, no surprises.

Identity comes from deck-slide[id], so URLs and presenter sync survive renumbering.

Title comes from <title>.

There is no slide list in deck.toml to keep in sync. Adding a file adds a slide.

deck — one slide is one complete HTML document Numbering with gaps of ten leaves room to insert; `deck add slide --after` picks the midpoint automatically.
